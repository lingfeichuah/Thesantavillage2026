Rules and notes

  • Check in at the entrance and keep your passport with you all night.
  • Be kind and respectful to the crew, vendors and fellow travellers.
  • Look after props and décor. Please don’t move or take them.
  • Ask before taking close-up photos of other people.
  • Outside food and drinks are not allowed in the hall.
  • Keep walkways and exits clear at all times.
  • Lost something? Ask at the check-in desk.
  • In an emergency, follow the committee’s instructions.
